## Failure modes

> **Compounding domains:** False authority couples security and legal exposure: a weakly grounded report may receive broader circulation and be relied upon in consequential decisions because it appears structured and authoritative.

### technical

- The staged pipeline can reliably preserve and amplify a weak SPOF: selection narrows ambiguity, critique improves explanation, and structured rendering makes the conclusion easier to consume without independent grounding.
- Deep Analysis agreement may measure consistency of compression rather than correctness of the selected hinge.
- Temporary job storage and browser-local history limit comparison of repeated outputs, making SPOF stability or sensitivity to prompt wording harder to observe.

### business

- If the method converts ambiguity into polished plausibility, users can obtain comparable premortems from general-purpose AI or internal review without paying.
- Founders and indie hackers may have occasional pre-build decisions, while product teams need a repeatable proposal-review workflow for subscription value.
- Deep Analysis increases processing and waiting burdens without necessarily improving selection.

### security

- Users may enter proprietary product concepts because the engine needs context, while jobs are temporarily held server-side and reports may be exported or shared.
- A weakly grounded analysis may circulate more widely because its structured form looks ready for organizational use.

### legal

- Users or teams may treat a confident SPOF and resilience profile as authoritative enough to reject a product, reallocate resources, or document a proposal decision.
- Confidentiality expectations may exceed the current storage model because browser-local history and temporary server-side jobs are not a clearly defined organizational record system.

### operations

- The product can reconnect a background job and complete valid JSON while still selecting the wrong hinge.
- Without permanent account-based history or an empirical validation loop tied to later decisions, it is difficult to track unstable or misleading SPOFs across English and Indonesian reports.
- Provider selection and pricing become operationally damaging when the product cannot demonstrate enough decision value to justify Standard and Deep processing burdens.
